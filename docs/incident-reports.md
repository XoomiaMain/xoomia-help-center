# Incident Reports

Learn how to document an Incident Report (DD102) in Xoomia.

---

## Overview

Xoomia allows authorized users to document and manage Incident Reports directly within the platform.

This guide walks you through the basic process of locating an Individual and accessing the Incident Report form.

---

## Before You Begin

Make sure you:

- Have an active Xoomia account.
- Have access to the Individual's record.
- Have permission to document an Incident Report.

---

## Documenting an Incident Report

### Step 1 — Log In

Log in to your Xoomia account.

### Step 2 — Navigate to the Individual

Locate the Individual who needs an Incident Report.

### Step 3 — Open Assigned Forms

Under the **Actions** column, select **View Assigned Forms**.

> **Tip:** If you do not see **View Assigned Forms**, select the **+** icon to view additional available actions.

### Step 4 — Locate the Incident Report

Locate the **Incident Report (DD102)** form.

Select the form to begin documenting the incident.

### Step 5 — Complete the Form

Enter all required information related to the incident.

Review the information carefully before submitting the form.

### Step 6 — Submit

Once all required information has been entered, submit the Incident Report.
